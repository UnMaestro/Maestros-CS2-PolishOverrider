# Polish Vanilla Theme Overrider 

Hello and Cześć. This is a **EU** and **NA** Vanilla Theme Overrider that turns Vanilla Signs, Roadmarkings and Decals to Polish ones.

## Functions
- Works **automaticly**, you don't need any knowledge whatsoever!
- Dynamic working Street Signs
- Dynamic working Bus/Tram Signs with max. 7 Lines displayed
- Bicycle Parking Spots
- 3D Model Signs

##  Requirements
- [Write Everywhere](https://mods.paradoxplaza.com/mods/92908/Windows) – required  

## Soft Requirements
- [Station Entrance Visuals by rodrigmatrix](https://mods.paradoxplaza.com/mods/95823/Windows) - for Detailed Station Line listing
